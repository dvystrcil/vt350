Bundles through a guide on each ceiling flap

Each bundle (PTFE tube plus Nitehawk cable) lies on a flap along the left or right of the ceiling and slides through a fixed, flared guide on the flap's inner edge. Only the free span from the guide down to the head is modelled: an elastic rod under its own weight, with the head end leaving straight up. Pick a configuration, then scrub or play the head along its path: out of its dock, then a 34 mm serpentine over the whole reach.

What it says

Z 550

The guide solves the sag

With a 0.2 N pull on the slack, no span dips below the head top at any stiffness from 700 to 11 000. With no take-up at all, the lowest is Z 502, about 54 mm above the gantry (Z 448). Without guides, the free loops hung to Z 408.

≈ 0.07 N

Friction alone won't pull slack back

400 mm of slack lying on the flap holds back about μ·w·L ≈ 0.3 × 5.8e-4 × 400 N (μ guessed). That is below the 0.2 N case, so expect the "none" rows unless something pulls: a light bungee or a hanging weight on each loop.

15–35 %

Spread the guides

One station per side makes the bundles fan out of eyelets 25 mm apart, and they touch under the flap at 70–100 % of positions. Spread along the edge (B), contact drops to 15–35 % for slots 1, 3 and 4, and 0–2 % for 2 and 5. It happens 0–90 mm below the flap, never down among the heads.

26–30 mm

Bend radius is tight only when soft

At EI 700 the tightest bend reaches the cable's rated 28 mm. From EI 2000 up it stays at 40 mm or more. This is now what your stiffness measurement mostly decides.

97°

The guide must be a trumpet

The span leaves the guide up to 97° from straight down, in any direction. The guide needs a flared lip of radius 28 mm or more, open all round, not a slot.

Assumptions and limits

  • Flaps 100 mm wide, inner edges at X −380 and −110; guides 5 mm under the ceiling (Z 685). Slots 1–2 on the left flap, 3–5 on the right.
  • Layout A: eyelets 25 mm apart around Y 200. Layout B: left Y 110 and 290; right Y 110, 200 and 290.
  • Weight 5.8e-4 N/mm and the EI values are estimates until your cantilever measurement.
  • Each head's O2S lock is taken to sit directly above its nozzle; docks at Y 10.9.
  • Each bundle is solved alone, so a "touch" means two bundles would push on each other. The slack's path on the flap and friction at the guide are not modelled.
  • Checks: exact gradients agree with central differences to 1e-9; a limp rod sags within 0.04 % of the analytic catenary. Code: cad/studies/flap_guide at 8eac49b (vt350#190); summary flap_guide_summary.json; discussion on vt350#3.
