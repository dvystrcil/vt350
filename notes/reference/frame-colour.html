Frame colour: the Fern look vs the powders you can buy

How the frame's powder-coat colour was chosen: renders of the frame in three greens, made fair by matching each powder's product photo, and a measured search of the vendor's catalogue. Tracking: vt350#101, sample order vt350#102.

Decision (2026-11-14): RAL 6011 Reseda, Prismatic gloss

Seen in the fair renders, under the chamber camera's lighting as well, RAL 6011 is the choice. It's in stock at DLLPDF, the candidate frame vendor. If a Fern-like powder becomes available (for example RAL 6001 Emerald by special order), it would be hard not to choose it instead. The physical sample from #102 still has the last word.

What the numbers say

Measured as lightness L, chroma C (how vivid) and hue h (CIELAB). RAL 6001 Emerald has Fern's lightness and hue and is a bit more vivid. RAL 6011 is lighter and duller. Fern sits between the two.

How these renders were made fair

  1. The scene's lighting and gloss make every frame colour render much lighter than its hex. Even a black frame renders #423F3C on the lit bar, because of reflections.
  2. So for each powder, the frame colour fed to Blender was adjusted, in six short test renders, until the lit middle front bar measured the same as the powder's product photo. RAL 6011 needs #27461F (bar renders #6E8C5D). RAL 6001 needs #013800 (bar renders #457A3A; blue stays 9 too high because the reflections can't get more vivid).
  3. The Fern column feeds #4F7942 straight in, which is how the render first liked was made. Its bar renders #9EB68F, much lighter than Fern itself.
  4. A product photo is one lit panel under the vendor's lights, so “matches the photo” is one reference, not the paint. The physical sample from #102 is the real test.

Measurement: a product photo's colour is the average of its centre 30 %; a render's is the average of a 300 × 8 px strip of the lit middle front bar in the outside view (x 700, y 415). Colour distance is CIE76 ΔE.
